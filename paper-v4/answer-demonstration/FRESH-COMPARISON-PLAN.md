## What the producer may learn from the repairs

Most rules already appear in the pinned skill's document-capture section. The
new checklist makes their application an explicit pass over the producer's own
draft, without identifying particular article passages or expected outputs:

> Read the complete declared source before settling the ontology. While capturing,
> interpret each passage in the context of the rest of that source and your own
> proposed records. Before submission, check the source against that draft for
> lost subjects, scope, attribution, qualifications and explicitly supported
> relationships. Keep distinct populations, observations, calculations and
> hypotheses distinct. A later qualification can change the interpretation of an
> earlier passage; preserve the connection and evidence for that interpretation.
> Co-occurrence does not justify a relationship. A reviewed-block count does not
> establish complete representation. Record unresolved meaning or missing schema
> capacity as a gap rather than inventing a value or weakening a claim's scope.

Do not prescribe article names, answer values, target records, desired predicates,
question topics, minimum record/relation counts or a passing coverage threshold.
The source and standard packs naturally contain domain terms and numbers; their
presence is not leakage. Exact input allowlisting and prompt identity protect
the delivery boundary. A manual audit must check the checklist's meaning; a word
blacklist cannot prove it is free of answer guidance.

This is a known development document. The coordinator has read it and its prior
results. Withholding questions and earlier artifacts from a fresh session does
not make the document held out, or prove absence from model training.

## A common reader is not automatically a fair reader

The current duration-query-01 program and SubjectGraphReads are the proposed
starting reader. Their exact method is
sha256:44109e65bc2c207cc194e47ebf4c3d1d2d288fc4d0af83efcdbf28aefbba0634.
They contain fixed type, field and predicate expectations. In particular,
SubjectGraphReads does not incorporate ontology_answers.text_binding; those two
previous improvements are not already a combined reader. The duration query also
requires the exact Instrument type. Merely hashing a new schema with binding.py
does not prove those expectations are satisfied.

Before launch, declare each program's schema dependencies and test them on
synthetic surfaces. Freeze algorithm, questions and compatibility rules. At
ontology acceptance, audit the new surface without reading any population, then
bind the exact accepted ontology and reader before phase two. Never request
ontology changes to satisfy the hidden questions.

Keep an absent declaration distinct from a declared but unpopulated field or
relation. An unsupported field spelling, subclass or predicate direction is a
reader/schema compatibility limitation, not proof of missing source capture.
Preserve any query's natural refusal; add paper-owned diagnostic accounting where
the current program silently returns no candidate. Unexpected incompatibility
must not trigger a post-population alias or wider selector. It can limit the
question comparison without invalidating an otherwise valid capture.

The full graph may be inspected separately to locate content that queries missed.
That diagnostic must not fill query answers. A mapping change or general reader
extension would be another declared intervention, not part of this fixed contrast.
No universal query planner or Core evaluation feature is required here.
