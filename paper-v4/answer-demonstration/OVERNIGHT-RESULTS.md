## Condition

Two fresh gpt-5.6-sol sessions independently populated Capture B's already fixed
ontology. The source reading, ontology/import closure, compiled population
surface, history profile and pinned acolyte skill were identical; only run
identifiers and workspace paths differed. Neither producer received prior
populations, competency questions, query code, answers, reviews or manuscripts.
No context was inherited. Access restrictions were instructions over shared
tools and files, not an operating-system sandbox.

Both packets and the original pilot-03 query bytes were frozen before producer
dispatch. Each producer could receive two structural diagnostic returns. Neither
needed one. After admission no producer was asked to improve its semantic result.
Two fresh independent reviewers saw their own exact source/ontology/query packet,
not the other result or producer logs. They reviewed every question and central
witness, preserving support, coverage and assembly as separate judgments.

This is a fixed-schema population condition, not a repetition of the Opus
ontology-acquisition runs and not a model ranking. There is no measured cost,
latency, token or equal-resource comparison. The task requires whole-reading
accounting but explicitly permits a partial capture; that permission is not a
claim that each omission was necessary.

## What the graphs contain, and what the reviews establish

01 stores 68 passage-sized ReportedClaim descriptions. It proposes no numeric
quantity fields, subject references or relations, and labels every retained
assertion STATED. Its helper chooses claim kinds with lexical rules. Many facts
are present in descriptions but unreachable by queries requiring a count, date,
quantity, Campaign, disposition or typed edge. A missing answer is therefore
often not missing text.

Five of its eight returned central witnesses are SUPPORTED. Three are PARTIAL:
their OTHER kind conflicts, under the reviewer's reading, with explicit method
justification or interpretation in their copied prose. Their scientific wording
is retained faithfully. This is a consequential classification judgment requiring
ratification, not an automatically detected scientific error. The whole-witness
rule prevents those rows from supplying coverage.

The four covered questions are CQ-T2-03 (extinct vent location), CQ-T4-03
(estimation assumption), CQ-T4-04 (qualified nonobservation of active vents) and
CQ-T5-05 (morphology and off-axis evidence). Each is ONE_ROW and answered by
stored prose. **COVERED is not typed graph composition.** CQ-T5-05 is a composition
question, yet its explicit argument resides in one description and the graph
has no relations. This is evidence that the review's coverage definition permits
prose-carried meaning, not that a graph join reconstructed the argument.

02 proposes eleven entities: three observations, two features, two materials,
two computational artifacts, one bibliographic source and one method. It returns
six row occurrences over two distinct witnesses, both SUPPORTED. These are the
RC2 primary-melt estimates based on Ba90 and Rb90. CQ-T3-02 and its paraphrase
CQ-C-05 are PARTIAL: bounds, unit and subject are present, but the returned
ESTIMATED value does not explicitly establish calculation rather than measurement
under the supplied definition. CALCULATED is retained in capture evidence, not
projected in these rows. The reviewer records that interpretive uncertainty.
CQ-T5-02 is also PARTIAL because only the RC2 side returns, with no RC3 quantity
or comparison relation. Supported quantities alone do not answer the comparison.

Both reviews account for all thirty questions, including unavailable-information
controls. 01 reviews nine row occurrences and eight witnesses; 02 reviews six
and two. Neither has unresolved witness locators. The absence vocabulary has
no dedicated unsupported-carrier reason: 01 explains its narrow NOT_MODELLED
usage rather than claiming that the graph lacks the rejected row's words.

## Gaps are declarations, not semantic proof

01's census reports 68 FULLY_FORMALIZED, zero PARTLY_FORMALIZED and 110
UNFORMALIZED assertions. The pinned Core counter derives those categories only
from whether an assertion has formalization targets and declared gaps. Copying
a whole block into description with no gap therefore counts as FULLY_FORMALIZED.
That does not certify decomposition into useful typed facts.

02 reports zero FULLY_FORMALIZED, six PARTLY_FORMALIZED and 172 UNFORMALIZED
assertions. Its 178 TYPE_ABSENT gap statements are generic. They do not prove
the schema lacks every omitted type. Original B uses this same compiled ontology
to express observations, hypotheses and relations absent here. Exact method
sequence predicates are a separate known schema/query limitation. Do not merge
that specific absence with unsubstantiated blanket gap declarations.

No generic Core read, admission or replay failure is demonstrated. Core correctly
preserves these proposals under its structural policy. The next adoption question
is how to require useful typed coverage and faithful qualifications without
feeding expected answers to a producer. That needs an explicit new condition,
not silently repairing these runs or changing the meaning of COVERED.
