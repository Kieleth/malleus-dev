## Proposed cases

One Cerebras architecture/performance document and one coherent NIST
post-quantum standards corpus would exercise different structures. The marine
paper remains the deep case. One three-document corpus counts as one additional
case, not three independent replications. See EXTRA-DOCUMENT-CANDIDATES.md for
versions, primary links, evidence-surface caveats and redistribution limits.

The point is to reveal portability failures, not raise a success percentage.
Two deliberately selected cases do not establish broad external validity. No
candidate has a measured token count exceeding one million. Testing documents
larger than a model's context would require a separately specified incremental
capture procedure; page count alone establishes neither that limit nor a
solution to it.

## Lean sequence

1. Confirm the two cases and exact versions. Freeze source bytes and the selected
   text readings using the existing path. Keep diagrams, equations or tables
   outside the reading visible as exclusions or capture gaps. Do not silently
   switch to OCR or add a multimodal ingestion method.
2. Freeze one producer condition and one pass per case, with the existing
   structural-diagnostic budget. Questions and answers remain outside ontology
   and population construction. For the corpus, declare source identity and
   ordering, one shared domain contract, and cross-document identity rules.
   Refuse or report a missing public multi-source capability, not a paper-local
   hidden merge. Do not require identical IDs across independently named facts.
3. Before seeing population outputs, bind six small checks from the accepted
   schema: a scoped fact, an explicit relation, a quantity with units and
   conditions, a qualified claim, a composition/reference question, and an
   unavailable-information control. For the corpus, the composition question
   must need records from more than one document. Freeze the actual questions
   and their requirements, not expected answer values.
4. Retain every terminal outcome. For an admitted population, reopen from the
   ledger, check replay equality, execute queries without source access, and
   trace their witnesses. A structural failure stays in the attempt table.
   No hand repair, answer-seeded population or replacement of a bad attempt.
5. One fresh LLM reviewer per case checks all six questions and every returned
   central witness against exact sources and vocabulary definitions. Keep model
   authorship explicit, ambiguities separate and unavailable answers visible.
   This is a smaller instrument, not a comparable thirty-question score.
6. Add a compact breadth table to the paper: attempted, admitted/refused,
   replay, query coverage, qualifier/relationship failures and evidence-surface
   exclusions. Stop the probe there unless a new author decision opens a
   specific correction experiment.
