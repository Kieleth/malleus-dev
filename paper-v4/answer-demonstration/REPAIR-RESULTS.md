the ERC agreement to S.C.S. The model removes the wrong attribution from the ERC
relation, preserving the correct agreement and Satish C. Singh as recipient.

mentions remain in the original capture and statement; this does not create a
complete structured account of all funders.

The evidence amendment faithfully represents one step of the authors' argument:
modelled CO2 saturation depth supports their preferred degassing hypothesis.

The new path returns neither of those observations. Related records already
exist, but remain unreached by this answer. The independent reviewer therefore
retains PARTIAL coverage. A source-supported relation can improve access without
making an answer sufficient. More edges are not a success criterion by themselves.
