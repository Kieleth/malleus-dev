## Argument

The completed paper asks what survives model proposal, structural admission,
replay and querying. Re-entry adds a second question: can a finding about
accepted knowledge produce a bounded amendment through that same admission
path, with exact evidence and the previous state preserved?

Query repair, capture amendment and ontology revision are different interventions.
The completed subject-aware query pass changes only reads. A future amendment
must change accepted state through a new proposal. An ontology revision changes
what can be represented and needs its own declared boundary. Do not bundle
these interventions into one unexplained before/after improvement.

## Minimum evidence for a re-entry result

The packet must identify the accepted base and contract, the initiating finding,
the permitted operation and evidence requirements, the exact candidate or typed
refusal, and its disposition. For acceptance, retain the resulting history and
replay receipt, the record-level change and preservation check, the query delta,
and the second invocation's outcome. Retain a failed or ambiguous result as such.
These are evidence within the existing identity groups, not a new collection of
paper-wide receipt chains.

Structural success alone is insufficient for a source-supported amendment.
The producer must be allowed to say that the source does not support the desired
relationship. No compulsory SUPPORTS edge, guessed subject, evaluator-authored
fact, direct graph write, or successful-effect claim from simulation is allowed.
An unchanged PDF supports correction of its representation, not a changed-world
claim. A trigger derived from evaluation is labelled evaluation-guided revision;
its answers do not replace the question-withheld first-pass result.
