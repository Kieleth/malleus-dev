## Why this is a real problem

NASA's Planetary Systems table retains separate published solutions. Its
Composite Parameters table combines references for completeness and explicitly
warns that the resulting parameter set may not be physically self-consistent.
This is a documented archive tradeoff, not an error we discovered.
[NASA table construction](https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/pscp_calc.html).

The archive exposes parameter definitions and reference information, and TESS
provides light curves, pixel products, instrument documentation and release
notes. These establish available source families, not yet a compatible packet
for a particular system.
[Parameter definitions](https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/API_PS_columns.html),
[TESS archive](https://archive.stsci.edu/missions-and-data/tess).

Astronomical provenance is established prior art. The International Virtual
Observatory Alliance already defines a provenance model covering datasets,
documents, activities and responsible agents. We must explain the additional
role, if demonstrated, of model proposals, checked acceptance and replayable
interpretation changes. Linking papers to numbers is not itself a new claim.
[IVOA Provenance Data Model](https://www.ivoa.net/documents/ProvenanceDM/).

## Decide what the semantic ledger records

The following are distinctions to assess, not an evaluator-authored ontology:

1. An astronomical object is not a measurement, a study or a parameter solution.
2. A paper's estimate is not a direct telescope observation or our calculation.
3. Equal units do not establish equal quantities or compatible assumptions.
4. A published claim can be retained without adopting it for our analysis.
5. Accepting a new analysis choice does not erase the earlier publication.

For example, the archive distinguishes mass from the radial-velocity minimum
mass, and identifies which quantity its best-mass column contains. A density
calculation must not silently treat these as interchangeable or treat a limit
as a central estimate. These are source-defined distinctions to preserve.
[Mass definitions](https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/API_PS_columns.html).

Initial admission records source-located assertions and the interpretation we
have explicitly accepted for a stated use. A later batch adds the second study.
Only an identified decision can change the selected interpretation, with its
reason and evidence. Conflicting or alternative published solutions remain
inspectable. Newer publication date alone is not an acceptance rule.

Keep observation time, publication date and import order separate. Preserve
missing precision and unknown times. A current archive export of an old study
is not a historical archive snapshot. Staged disclosure of papers is a controlled
experiment, not a reconstruction of what the scientific community knew then.

The exact Core history profile and representation of the selection decision
remain to be verified before launch. Do not invent protocol events or claim
Semantic Re-entry merely because another source was imported.

## Four separate questions, four evidence records

| Question | What must establish it | What does not establish it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can the model construct a useful ontology? | Source-grounded assessment of whether the selected domain distinctions can be expressed, with concrete missing distinctions named. | Compilation, number of classes, or a schema tailored to the evaluation questions. |
| Can it populate that ontology faithfully? | Inspection of proposed values and relationships against exact table cells and passages, including attribution, qualification and omissions. | Well-formed records or resolving a source locator. |
| Can Malleus preserve accepted changes? | Admission tests, unchanged old history, reconstruction from retained history, and reproduction of both accepted states. | A plausible graph or model agreement. |
| Can consumers retrieve and compose the information? | Fixed tasks answered from declared graph/evidence surfaces, checked calculations, and complete source-grounded review before and after the update. | Relation counts, fluent prose, or a correct number from an incompatible combination. |

Structural compliance, faithfulness and intended-use coverage receive separate
findings. Ontology assessment is diagnostic, not a hidden repair loop. If a
missing distinction prevents the task, preserve that result and propose a
separate repair rather than silently changing the ontology or questions.

### 3. Build and inspect the first accepted state

Expose only the initial source packet to the producer. Verify actual model,
settings and input delivery, not the producer's self-report. Public plan text,
questions, later-study material and earlier outputs must not leak into the
producer's tools or inputs. This is controlled delivery, not a claim that the
model's pretraining excludes the science.

Compile, retain attempts, apply the declared acceptance process, then reopen and
replay. Perform source-grounded assessment separately. Do not silently relabel
structural acceptance as scientific endorsement. Unsupported proposals do not
become selected scientific inputs merely by compiling; partial coverage stays
visible. Evaluators do not author replacement facts.

Two consumers use this same accepted state: a deterministic calculation and a
fresh LLM explanation. Each declares its evidence access. A source-assisted
answer is not reported as graph-only. Parameter lookup must be typed and
traceable, not a reader hardcoding planet IDs or expected values.

Deliverable: the initial graph, calculation, explanation, complete assessment
and a reproducible history. A failed or partial acquisition remains a result,
not an automatic permission to launch another sample.

### 4. Introduce one real change and reuse the accepted model

A fresh continuation session receives the accepted ontology/state, usable

This is a cross-session continuation, not a second independent end-to-end

Append to the existing history under the frozen rules. Repeat both consumers

Deliverable: one before/after example with exact supporting evidence and retained

### 5. Compare, diagnose and decide what belongs in the paper

Use a fresh-context reference that receives the same stage's sources, questions

make no advantage claim over databases or provenance systems.

Assess all fixed tasks, not only attractive examples. Attribute each failure to

Deliverable: four-question results table, full task assessment, source-backed

## Evaluation: small and complete

Propose six positive tasks and two controls, finalized after source selection
but before any producer output. They should cover quantity meaning, uncertainty,
coherent parameter selection, observational evidence, calculation and change.
At least one requires prose plus table information, not table lookup alone.
The full source packet must be checked before claiming a control is unanswerable.
An empty query does not establish absence from either the source or the graph.

Keep these evaluator-only. The producer receives domain sources and generic
acquisition instructions, not questions or question-derived semantic hints.
After ontology acceptance, bind queries to types and properties without supplying
answers to the producer. Before the update, freeze the reader, formulas, units
and output comparison method. Fixing retrieval later is a separate condition.

For every task, retain required meanings, returned evidence, calculation inputs,
assessment and a plain-English explanation of full, partial, unsupported or
unanswerable results. Numeric checks use declared units, constants, precision
and tolerances, not JSON exact-match as semantic evaluation. Do not invent
independent errors, covariance or propagated uncertainty when sources do not
justify them. Where necessary report only the conditional central calculation
and explain why its uncertainty cannot be derived.

Model-assisted reviews name the actual assessor. Human ratification stays marked
pending until Luis or an identified human performs it. Account separately for
initial construction, review, querying and update work, including failed attempts.
One worked case cannot establish a general accuracy, cost or memory advantage.
