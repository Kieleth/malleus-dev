## Smallest reproduction boundary still to settle

The five-file archive reproduces the typeset paper, not the experiment. Checking
retained answers needs the query program, ontology/import closure, retained
ledger and replay inputs, plus their existing identity bindings. Checking the
semantic judgments additionally needs the exact reading, locator convention,
question requirements, vocabulary definitions and completed review records.
Running a fresh producer is a third task and need not reproduce the same graph.

No such evidence bundle is exported here. Before any public packaging, Luis
must settle access to source-bearing inputs. A source-acquisition recipe cannot
be called sufficient until it reconstructs the selected reading and locators
from the pinned source and tool. Redacted or omitted evidence must be declared;
it cannot silently substitute for the frozen inputs. Keep these release choices
separate from the already completed source-only build check.

The selected source is [Yu et al. (2025)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-55792-9).
The candidate carries attributed excerpts already selected for the appendix,
not the complete article. Additional publication access and human ratification
are unresolved author decisions. No Core or Robotics capability is needed to
make those decisions.
