## Meaning to investigate

The accepted graph is a previous interpretation, not proof of correct meaning.
Inspect all retained assertions behind a record, not just its preferred locator.

The scope file identifies three problems and the old records you may change.
It identifies passages and questions about the representation, not replacement
values. For each problem, decide what the source supports, what the existing
