## Bounded representation proposal, E-0396

Prepared for discussion, not applied to the ontology or graph. The question is
what each record means, not how to make the earlier assessor accept its label.

### The underlying problem

The source's layout has become the model's unit of meaning in these cases.
A copied block is retained evidence, but it can contain a broken sentence,
publication metadata and a scientific interpretation. Giving that whole block
one claim type and one function leaves the meaning of the classification unclear.
The opposite shortcut, splitting every sentence, also fails when a subject or
qualification occurs elsewhere. The unit should be a coherent proposition or
metadata item, with all context needed to understand it.

There is a second problem. `EarthScienceClaimKind` has twelve undefined labels.
They describe several things: a finding, a role in an argument, a limitation,
or publication administration. `assertion_modality` separately expresses how
the source presents an assertion. A method-justification label is not a
certainty label; a statement printed in a figure is not a scientific category.
Definitions must expose any genuine overlap before a scalar field forces a
choice. Do not invent another catch-all category for captions.
Keep source qualification on the relevant proposition, not on the whole block.
STATED records how a source presents content; it is not an assertion of truth.

Here, a meaning unit is not a ledger transaction. The source-assertion profile
can still accept a capture as one atomic batch containing many records and
evidence excerpts. Separating their meanings does not require one transaction
per sentence or per assertion, and capture order is not the assertions' domain
time.

### Proposed category meanings

These are a small discussion vocabulary for the identified cases, not a frozen
definition of all twelve labels. Apply a category to the proposition it qualifies,
not to the containing block, section or document. The model must identify any
remaining ambiguity before population, rather than silently choose a broader
meaning. Human acceptance of these definitions would not itself validate a
particular scientific classification.

| Category | Proposed assertion about the content | Does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BACKGROUND | Provides scientific context for the study's discussion. | That arbitrary surrounding metadata is a scientific proposition, or that the contextual statement is true. |
| OBSERVATIONAL_RESULT | Reports an outcome of an identified observation or analysis, with its subject and scope. | That it is directly observed rather than calculated, that it is this paper's own result, or that it is certain. |
| MECHANISTIC_INTERPRETATION | Offers an interpretation of the physical process accounting for an observation. | An observed causal event or experimentally established causation. |
| METHOD_JUSTIFICATION | Gives a reason for choosing or trusting a method. | That any text in Methods, any procedure description, or any figure legend is a justification. |
| ATTRIBUTION | Assigns a statement or contribution to an identified person or work. | The content of that statement, or an evidential argument supporting it. |

Some propositions can serve more than one function. If that occurs, a producer
must report the overlap; it must not cut away context to satisfy a single label.
Whether to use a declared primary function or a separate multivalued role field
is an ontology decision if the bounded cases actually require it. No such field
is proposed or added now. Publication identity should use the existing source
type rather than being squeezed into a scientific BACKGROUND claim. A proposition
about publication history can still be a proposition; it must be distinguished
from the publication object and from earth-science content.

### What the existing mechanisms permit

living Core checkout. They compile in memory and do not retain or admit toy data.

One block supplies separate observation and report-name excerpts. A second block
qualifies the observation. One contextual graph statement has derivations from

adapter preserves the exact capture bytes and the links from fields to excerpts.
Joining the excerpts and falsely locating them as one quotation refuses with
NOT_VERBATIM. This establishes legal representation shape, not that a model can
select faithful propositions or that the compiler checks natural-language meaning.

A second test adds only a synthetic enum description in memory. The compiled
structural facts stay identical, while source/annotation evidence and artifact
bytes change. Definitions must remain covered by the exact ontology-source
identity in successor producer and reviewer packets. A matching compiled fact digest alone
does not establish that both operators received the same meanings. This is not
a reason to make Core decide scientific truth. No description-only history
revision or semantic enforcement is claimed by this test.

The same public-path probe establishes a separate history constraint. A
same-type replacement plus additional records compiles. Changing an old
EarthScienceClaim directly into a ResearchArticle refuses with
SUPERSESSION_TYPE_MISMATCH; making two records both replace that one old record
refuses with SUPERSESSION_FORK. The accepted ledger stays byte-identical.
Consequently, an arbitrary split or reclassification is not an authorized or
mechanically available repair under this frozen replacement path. Choosing one
new claim as the old record's continuation is a semantic decision, not a way to
work around the refusal. Do not use dummy replacement claims, silently remove
history, or leave the faulty record current and call the repair complete.

The current ontology also lacks a dedicated date-only publication-acceptance
field. Do not reinterpret `created_at`, substitute the publication year, or invent
a time of day to fill that gap. Retaining the source date as text preserves
evidence but is not a typed date representation. Define the intended field and
check compiler support before proposing that addition.

### Conditions before turning this into a repair

The design is useful but is not accepted wholesale for population.

First, keeping source bytes is not the same as preserving graph-accessible
meaning. The design allows some publication metadata to remain evidence-only.
That would be an incomplete graph correction where the old mixed claim exposed
it as prose. A candidate must account for those meanings explicitly; retaining
the source alone cannot support a no-information-loss claim.

Second, choose classifications by their defined meaning. Being reported through
another work does not by itself decide MEASURED versus STATED. Likewise, the
caption's approximation must not silently become a qualification of a different
meaning unit. The source-estimate and schematic-use distinctions must stay intact.

Third, the proposed Core requirement is conditional. The designer recommends
replacing the bibliography claim with a publication identity. The author must
choose whether the old record instead remains a meaningful citation assertion,
or is an erroneous identity to withdraw. Keeping a dummy claim just to satisfy
same-type supersession is unacceptable; a deliberately modelled assertion needs
its own justification. The pass does not prove that every faithful design needs
a new Core operation, and living Core was not inspected or contacted.

Finally, the current general ResearchRelation includes REPORTED_BY, whereas
EvidenceRelation is described as support or challenge. The structural enum does
not enforce that narrower description. An attribution design must name the
appropriate class and meaning, not rely on the fact that an enum value compiles.

The suggested civil-date slots, citation type, figure-convention subtype,
multivalued functions and estimate-use reference remain optional proposals.
Recommendation: freeze only the declarations needed by the selected correction,
with an information-preservation check for each affected meaning. Do not add the
whole list or launch another full capture from this design alone. A selected
history gap would first require a read-only current-Core check, then coordination
with its owner if still missing.
