# G1: specimens with exact expected answers

24 September 2026. Gate G1 of [GEDANKENEXPERIMENTS-01.md](../GEDANKENEXPERIMENTS-01.md):
a small, independently authored specimen per obligation, with exact expected
reads and refusals, written before any encoder or Core change.

Role: the specimens are `CONFORMANCE_FIXTURE` candidates under an
`OPTIONAL_PROFILE` (temporal semantic history). Nothing here is a
`PROTOCOL_INVARIANT`. Answer states and refusal categories are draft test
vocabulary, not Core enums. Persistence, authority and sufficiency decisions
are `ADOPTER_CHOICE` and stay listed as open choices below.

Expected answers were written by hand from the design documents. They were not
generated by running code. The validator checks form, references, positions and
copies from the reused answer key; it cannot check that an answer is the right
meaning.

## Open choices for Luis

Each is written in its specimen with the answer under every branch where a
query depends on it. None is decided here.

1. **Account authority for unscoped reads** (OC-03-USE-SELECTION-SCOPE, CA08).
   A selection of Q2 for a named use either binds only that use (unscoped stays
   ambiguous) or an adopter-declared default authority resolves unscoped reads (Q2).
2. **Assumptions in unscoped candidates** (OC-03-ASSUMPTIONS-IN-UNSCOPED, CA05).
   Unscoped ambiguity lists reports only, or every accepted account including
   the assumption.
3. **Reconsideration flag** (OC-06-FLAG-SOURCE, AJ07). Derived from declared
   premise links, or present only once a reviewer records it.
4. **Sufficiency of a remaining argument** (OC-06-SUFFICIENCY, AJ09). No
   declared rule (C1 stays, sufficiency not assessed) or a declared rule that
   any unrevised route suffices.
5. **Persistence rule selection** (OC-07-RULE-SELECTION, VQ04). An accepted rule
   applies by default within its scope, or only when the caller names it.
6. **Persistence rule acceptance** (OC-07-RULE-ACCEPTANCE). Must the rule be
   accepted in the ledger before use, or may a caller supply an unaccepted rule.
7. **Completeness authority** (OC-07-COMPLETENESS-AUTHORITY). Who declares the
   completeness assumption, and whether it is bounded to a period.
8. **Initial state in a persistence rule** (OC-07-INITIAL-STATE). Whether a rule
   may declare a state before the first recorded event.
9. **Scope of a missing artifact** (OC-08-WITHHELD-SCOPE, ST11). Only reads
   whose closure needs it refuse, or the whole rebuild refuses.
10. **How a transition closes a report** (OC-01-TRANSITION-CLOSES-REPORT). The
    transition names its target, or closure is inferred from account and start.
11. **Group membership on member lookup** (OC-05-GROUP-OR-MEMBER). Returned by
    default or on request.

## Notes on the reused answer key

No error was found in expected-views-v1.json. Three observations, none changed:

- Its K3 history entry lists visible cells but no metadata. PH-K3-META in
  g1-01 adds the K3 knowledge periods (a2 ends at K3) as a new authored query.
- It uses `NO_APPLICABLE_ACCOUNT` (P08), a state distinct from "no accepted
  account". The G1 state list keeps it.
- Its consumer expectations C01 to C07 are prose requests. G1 gives each a
  concrete query and binds only the fields the key states. C03 and C04 are
  bound to the order case and C07 to the competing-accounts case, as outcome
  checks only.

g1-01 adds positions K1a and K1b (model and execution) between K1 and K2.
The answer key's labels keep their meaning; K2 is still the transition.

## What these specimens cannot establish

- That any encoder, graph or SQLite, gives these answers. That is G2.
- Core admission, actual checks, replay or atomicity. A refusal here is an
  expected outcome, not an observed one. A storage transaction that rolls back
  is not proof of Core admission atomicity.
- That the answers are the only correct meaning. They are one authored reading
  of the design documents, open to Luis's correction.
- General temporal reasoning: uncertain transition windows, late and
  future-effective reports beyond the valve case, interval splitting,
  retraction, recurrence, clock mappings and episode ids are not covered.
- GE-01 (marine interpretation versions), GE-08 (ontology and rule changes),
  GE-10 withdrawal and current-view hiding, and the Shop ordering, shipment and
  receipt analogue of GE-07 have no specimen.
- Any claim about the real article. g1-05 is synthetic and only shaped after
  reading block page:5:block:006, whose bytes were not read.
- Performance, volume or workload behaviour.
