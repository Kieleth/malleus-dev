## What the review found that the cell did not measure

Two questions PARTIAL, for reasons the producer's modelling choices made:
- CQ-01: the observation network is never a record; it lives in a count's free-text `count_scope` and in the instrument-type record. Run-13's producer made the network an Instrument record with a name and tags.
- CQ-04: no projected field of any row carries the extensional-stress condition. It sits in a claim's statement, reachable through the capture and dropped by the Claim projection. Run-13's producer put it in the claim's name. With `Claim.statement` left empty under the licence (both producers' choice, Luis's ruling pending), a claim row's content is its name, and responsiveness on a claim question depends on how the producer names its claims. This is the sharpest finding of the cell: the protocol withholds the one field that carries a claim's content, and the review then judges the row on the label the producer minted for it. The "no minted labels" candidate and this finding pull in opposite directions; the ruling on `statement` decides which way.

Twenty-two PARTIAL rows in 919, none UNSUPPORTED: one unstated qualifier (north-south on the Chain transform, four rows), one kind the block does not support (a massif as a volcanic edifice, three rows), one bare ratio the block gives as approximate, one work kind read from an entry's form that does not carry it, one initial in an uncited block, a database as an organization, "west of" flattened to "located in", a description joining two captions, and nine subject-fit rows.

Rows: 919 against run-13's 515, from the flat surface. The type sets, a translation of run-13's, put BibliographicWork (the surface's one type for datasets and for the 84 references) in two questions and GeologicFeature (the one type for every feature) in three; 484 of the 919 rows are witnesses already returned by an earlier question, 44 are the same witness re-projected under a parent type inside one question.
