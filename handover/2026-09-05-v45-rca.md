## 3. The change held on the return and failed on coverage

No SUBJECT_NOT_NAMED refusal: the first cell with no return at either stage. The producer tagged 27 entities with the forms the reading uses, and its own validator carried the name-or-tag rule. Coverage nonetheless fell from 49 to 37 per cent. Measured: 77 source-asserted records without a subject sit on formalizing sentences that contain the name or a tag of a capture entity, so the check would have accepted them. The producer treated attachment as optional once the check no longer refused; the rule "carries that thing as its subject" is stated as a duty and behaves as a preference without a gate. This is the third time in the loop that a stated rule with no mechanism did not move a producer (coverage in run-05, subject attachment in run-08, attachment again here).

## 5. What the review surfaced in the harness

- One reviewer reported that RELATION rows project an empty relation record. Checked against the frozen query results after the entry was written: false. Run-11's 35 relation rows all carry `relation_type` (21 also `spatial_qualifier`, 4 `contribution_role`), and run-10's 16 all carry `relation_type`; the projector omits only fields the relation record does not have (`name`, `strength`). The reviewer read a `record` key that relation rows do not carry. No harness defect here; the reviewer's rows were judged on the identity triple by the reviewer's own choice, and that is recorded in the rationales.
- 9 SUBJECT_NOT_IN_BLOCK rows where the block uses the abbreviation and the row projects the full name: the tag matched at capture and is not projected at query. Either project `tags` beside `name` in SUBJECT rows, or the reviewer reads the tag; the former is one field.
- The Instrument ENTITY case returns SoftwareTool records under the Instrument projection (a subtype), duplicating them under their own case. Type-only queries include subtypes; the binding should not list a type and its subtype in one set, or the projector should report the concrete type. Small.
- One feature type on the surface makes the three science questions return identical SUBJECT and RELATION rows (86 and 9 each): 190 of 457 rows were judged three times. A binding that dedups identical (record, subject) pairs across questions and judges once would have cost a third less. Or, less: a question's rows are a set; the same row judged once carries its label to every question that returns it.
