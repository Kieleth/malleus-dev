## Reading

1. Admission is stable. Five producers of one condition, four admitted at runner attempt 1 with no return, one at attempt 2. Support saturates in every cell (97 to 100 percent of witnesses SUPPORTED); the PARTIAL residue is text-layer boundaries (run-23) or the producer's typing beyond the sentence (run-24), never a contradicted claim.
2. The variance is modelling. The same reading under the same skill produced 55, 26, 31, 22 and 21 relations; 77, 94, 15, 20 and 153 declared gaps; 393 to 333 assertions. Run-24's producer declared 137 TYPE_ABSENT gaps and gave 48 records a subject; run-23's declared 6 and gave 96. Both are honest under the skill; they are different modellers.
3. Coverage settles once the evaluator's reach is fixed. Under v4.13 the UNREACHED_RECORD cause is gone in both cells (11 to 0 to 0) and coverage sits at 92 and 90 of 102. What remains splits between the licence (WITHHELD 2 and 7: the answer is in the sentence the graph holds as a digest) and the ontology (NOT_MODELLED 8 and 5: no slot for an acceptance date, no evidence or comparison relation, no subject on a quantity).
4. The controls now behave: five of five under v3.1. The two paraphrase controls matched in all three v3 cells; the two not-in-source controls read NONE in all three; the excluded-surface control needed its in-text semantic removed.
5. Two reviewer deviations of the same kind (run-22, run-24 read the expected outcomes before judging) and one template defect (the locator form in the v3 task) are on the record for the ratifier and for the next protocol revision.

## What to write, and what not to claim

The paper can report: five cells of one producer condition on one document, admission at first or second attempt in all, support saturated under two review protocols, coverage of 82 to 92 of 102 required semantics under the thirty questions once the query reach was fixed, absences attributable by cause, and a control set that behaves. It cannot claim: that any label is ratified (Luis ratifies), that the numbers generalise beyond one document and one producer family, that the graphs answer questions a reader would ask beyond the thirty, or that the harness fix separates from the evaluator's sets in run-23 (E-0343's offline measurement is the kind's own evidence).
