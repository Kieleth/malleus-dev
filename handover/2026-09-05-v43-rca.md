## 3. The change under test held

The hypothesis was that the same model attaches its quantities and claims to named things once the pack has a place for it, and that a value-blind query then reaches them without relation records. Measured: CQ-03's depth and CO2 quantities come back as SUBJECT rows attached to RC2 and RC3 (14 depth, 12 CO2), CQ-02 returns 50 subject rows about RC2, CQ-04 returns the five dispositioned claims attached to the mantle and the lithosphere, and every one of the 326 SUBJECT rows was judged local and SUBJECT_IN_BLOCK. Run-08 had the same quantities unattached and unreachable. Responsiveness moved from P, N, N, N to R, P, R, P, and the two PARTIALs are the two limits already known from run-05 and run-04: the position relative to the ridge axis lives in prose, and the mechanism's interior steps carry uneven epistemic weight.

Against run-04's honest baseline of 146 local-relation rows: run-09 has 405 structural rows (SUBJECT plus RELATION), all local, with 7 PARTIAL among them. Against run-08's 8: fifty-fold.

## 4. What the subject check cost and taught

Runner attempt 1 refused 98 of 129 subjects. The producer had named entities by the reading's descriptive introduction and asserted about them by the reading's designator. The check was right; the skill had never said what a name is. The repair (32 renames, 15 re-pointed, 23 dropped) cost 37,536 tokens and one return, and produced names that read as the source's own in every row. One skill sentence removes the return for the next producer.

## 5. The ENTITY flood

1,061 of 1,466 rows came from the ENTITY kind, which returns every admitted record of every type in a question's set. Their support was 1,052 of 1,061, so they are not wrong; they are not answers. They tripled the review: 1.21 million tokens across four sessions, against 322 thousand for run-04. Computed offline from the record already judged: a binding that keeps ENTITY only for types that carry no subject, and reaches every source-asserted record through its subject, returns 630 rows (58, 319, 131, 122 per question) with 618 SUPPORTED and 12 PARTIAL, and no row of it is unjudged. That is a removal, it makes the coverage census bite (an unattached observation then reaches nothing), and it needs no new producer cell to validate.

## 6. A new drift the review surfaced

Twenty of run-09's 212 source-asserted records carry an `assertion_modality` that no formalizing assertion carries (MEASURED in the capture, STATED on the record, twelve times). Run-04 and run-08 had zero. The record's modality is a copy of the assertion's, set by the producer a second time, and this cell's producer tagged the clause where the capture tagged the sentence. The reviewers judged all twenty on the prose and flagged them. The elegant correction is one source of truth: a record's modality is the formalizing assertion's; if a sentence carries two modalities it is two assertions. One adapter check now; removing the slot from the record later, once the query projects the assertion's modality through the trace.

## 7. Two returns that recur

Both Opus cells on the current skill spent their first ontology attempt on a `date` or `uri` range (the elaborator binds five seed scalar types; the skill says every built-in loads), and this cell spent its first runner attempt on names. Neither is a rule gap; both are the protocol not stating what it already checks. The refusal should name the bound ranges; the skill should say what a name is.
