# RCA: what changed between run-04 (v4.1) and run-08 (v4.2), from the data

Ordered by Luis on 2026-09-05 after run-08 returned 8 query rows to run-04's 240. Every number below was computed from the two cells' populations, captures, query results and the frozen reading with one script per table; the reading is not quoted beyond one clause the analysis turns on. Locality is the Core-12 definition: a relation is LOCAL when every block that formalizes one of its own fields also formalizes a field of at least one endpoint.

## 1. What changed in the inputs

Same model (Opus 5), same reading, same questions, same eight-input isolation. Three declared inputs moved: metrology 0.2.0 to 0.3.0 (value qualification), research 0.2.0 to 0.4.0 (CRediT roles; `hypothesis_disposition` declared evaluative), and the skill (Core-9: output file separated from the adapter call, verbatim method, block ids from the inventory, honest-citation rule, aggregated locator refusals; Core-12: the statement digest is recomputed, a disposition comes from the disposing sentence, a relation's endpoints are named by the formalizing assertion and an implied relation is a `RELATION_ABSENT` gap). Core at run time gained DIGEST_MISMATCH, EVALUATIVE_SLOT_NOT_EVALUATED, UNKNOWN_ASSERTION_LOCATOR and the derivation census. The harness gained the stop-rule sentence, chained-cause diagnostics, the interpreter preflight, the binding frozen at acceptance and the v2 log.

## 3. The same sentence in both cells

The clause that answers CQ-02 and half of CQ-03 sits in one block on page 2: the deep microseismicity at about 10 to 20 km lies beneath the ridge axis of segment RC2, beside two sibling clauses for the shallow and the normal-depth groups.

- Run-04 made three `EarthquakeSet` entities (deep, shallow, normal-depth) and three `LIES_BENEATH` relations to the named structures, all LOCAL: the sentence names both endpoints and the producer's ontology had an entity type for an earthquake population and a predicate for "beneath".
- Run-08 made three depth `Observation` records (10 to 20 km APPROXIMATE; 4 to 10 km; 0 to 6 km) whose `quantity_kind` text carries the location ("depth of deep microseismicity beneath the ridge axis of the segment RC2"), and no entity for the earthquake groups and no relation. The value is right, the hedge is right, the provenance is right, and the subject is a substring.

The Core-12 rule did not forbid the relation: the sentence names both endpoints. What removed it was run-08's modelling. Its ontology has no earthquake-population entity; its `GeospatialRelation` is defined as a predicate "between two named Earth features"; it deferred research predicates to the pack ("ResearchRelationType already names CLAIM_CONCERNS, OBSERVED_WITH, ...") and then used none of them; and the pack itself has no predicate or slot for "this observation is about that thing". Under a pack-first reading of the skill, every number became an `Observation`, and an observation is not a named Earth feature, so it could be the endpoint of nothing the producer had defined. The path of least resistance was the free-text `quantity_kind`, which the skill says to keep in the source's words.

## 4. Where run-04's rows came from

Of run-04's 240 rows, 94 rested on NON_LOCAL relations: 11 of its 13 `CONCERNS_FEATURE` links, all 5 `CLAIM_CONCERNS`, 71 of 75 `CITES`, all 7 `CONTRIBUTED_TO`, and a scattering of others, most of them hung on two hub sentences that name none of the endpoints (the run-04 review RCA). The reviewer supported those rows on the reading, so the answers were right; the evidence pointers were not. For the paper's thesis, which is that every admitted record traces to the assertion and block it came from, 39 per cent of run-04's rows are the weak evidence, not the strong. The 146 LOCAL rows are the honest baseline run-08 should be measured against, and against that baseline run-08 still holds 8.

## 5. Verdict

Progression on everything the protocol can check: acceptance and admission at first attempt, no returns, no hubs, hedges carried, digests recomputed, dispositions from disposing sentences, more derived fields, more quantities, lower cost, and a binding that provably never saw a row. Regression on the one thing the protocol does not check and the query surface depends on: whether an observation or a claim is attached to what it is about. Run-04 had that attachment 34 times honestly and 21 times by mis-attribution; run-08 has it 23 times as text inside `quantity_kind` and zero times as structure. The v4.2 changes did not cause the loss; they removed the escape (hub derivation) that had hidden the absence of a rule, and a different modelling choice by the same model did the rest. One cell each, so the size of the effect is not measured; the mechanism is on disk.

## 6. What follows, for decision

1. **Subject as a first-class derived element**, at the pack level, not as a producer instruction: a `subject` reference on the `SourceAsserted` mixin (observations, claims, asserted ratios) to the entity the assertion is about, formalized by the same assertion, with a mechanical check in the adapter that the subject entity's name occurs in the formalizing statement (whitespace-collapsed), a typed refusal when it does not, and a census axis for subject coverage. This is the anchors point in its concrete form: connected by definition, at the fundamental level, checkable, and cheaper than a relation record. An observation with no nameable subject is a gap the census counts.
2. **Entity-level query cases**: a case kind that projects a type's fields with no relation, and one that follows the subject reference, both type-only and value-blind. Either would have returned run-08's 29 depth and 15 CO2 observations for CQ-03.
3. **One skill sentence**: a quantity or claim reported about a named thing carries that thing as its subject; the subject is never folded into `quantity_kind` text.
4. Then v4.3 and a run-09 Opus cell, measured against run-04's 146 LOCAL rows, not its 240.
