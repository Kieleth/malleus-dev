## The mechanical layer is clean; what is left is variance

Three Opus cells at a stable Core (v4.7 to v4.9 differ only in the word rule and the executor): no UNSUPPORTED row in 1,867 rows, every digest verified, every non-local relation still supported on its own sentence, PARTIAL shares of 3.5, 2.4 and 1.4 per cent. The returns at the runner or gate are all producer-side (a root field, modality defaults, a glued form). The responsiveness labels move: R R R R, P R R P, P R P P. Read row by row, three sources of movement, none of them Core:

1. **Producer modelling.** Run-13 made the observation network an Instrument record with tags; runs 14 and 15 left it a bare name or a count's scope, and CQ-01 fell to PARTIAL twice. Run-13 named the claim so its row carried the extensional-stress condition; runs 14 and 15 did not, and CQ-04 fell to PARTIAL twice. With `Claim.statement` empty under the licence (all three producers' choice), a claim row's content is its name. The same skill, three sessions, three pre-flight sets: run-13 built a byte slicer and a validator and asked nothing; run-14 probed the ambient PATH, asked three questions and missed the modality equality; run-15 refused to cite from recall, asked nothing, missed the root-field set and caught its own reference-parser error before writing.
2. **Reviewer thresholds.** The same qualification on CQ-03, competing intervals with nothing marking the central pairing, was read as a precision cost by run-13's reviewer (RESPONSIVE) and as material ambiguity by run-15's (PARTIAL). Both stated their line. The aboutness line likewise: each session wrote where it drew it and how many rows a stricter reading would move (about forty in run-14, about thirty in run-15).
3. **Reachability and nesting in the type-only binding.** A subject-bearing record without a subject has no ENTITY case (v4.4), so `obs:pore_pressure_trigger`, the record CQ-04's key sentence formalizes, is unreachable by any question. And CQ-04's set of types is a subset of CQ-03's, so CQ-04's 147 witnesses are CQ-03's 154 less seven, byte-identical: the binding carries the question's semantics only through the type set, by design, and nested sets give nested answers.

Named is not about, fourth cell: of the 18 attachable records, 14 name a tool or model. The count is now small because the producer sets most subjects itself; the shape has not changed since run-12.

## Producer behaviour worth the paper's appendix

Run-15 took the `none_found` form on three grounding blocks, naming the vocabularies it recalled (GeoSciML, QuakeML, GeoSPARQL, SWEET, RCC-8, the IUGS-CGI registers) and refusing to cite their URLs unverified; runs 13 and 14 cited from recall and the overseer verified after the fact, once with two URLs unreachable on transport. Run-15 recorded funding as a gap because the closed ResearchRelationType has no funding predicate, rather than bend CONTRIBUTED_TO; it typed each hypothesis's disposition on the sentence that disposes of it; it caught and corrected its own reference-parser error (a phantom entry from a URL tail, two dropped entries) before writing, and left the 79 references unnamed rather than parse titles out of damaged entries. Its cost was the lowest of the three.

## A harness defect found at the merge

The review validator accepted only query-result v2 and refused run-15's v3 record; Paper-17 bumped the schema without touching the validator, and run-15's tests did not run the validator on a real record. Two lines changed at the merge; recorded in E-0169.

## Where the loop stands

Seven iterations: three clarifications of an existing check (ranges and names, tags, words), two removals (the ENTITY flood, projection), one executor removal (rows per witness), two additions that held (the subject element, one source of truth for modality), one projection that failed. The last three cells found no Core defect; what they found is producer variance, reviewer threshold, and two properties of the type-only binding that are design choices, not defects. The next single change the evidence supports is not in Core. Three things are Luis's:

- **`Claim.statement`** under CC BY-NC-ND: filled, and claim rows carry their content; empty, and responsiveness on claim questions depends on the producer's naming.
- **The aboutness and material-ambiguity lines** for the reviewers, so that a label means the same thing across cells.
- **Review protocol v3**, one judgement per distinct witness distributed to every question's rows: 433 rows become 162 judgements here, 919 become 391 in run-14, with nothing lost, since a support label is a property of the row and its block.

And one recommendation: with Core stable since dc52547 and the harness at v4.9, the matrix cells (Sonnet 5, Haiku 4.5) at this protocol are what the paper needs next; a further Core change now would restart comparability across the three Opus cells.
