## 3. The change under test held, and so did the two clarifications

Modality drift 0 of 174 against 20 of 212. The adapter's new refusal never fired: the producer's own pre-flight carried the skill sentence. No INVALID_RANGE return, after two cells that each spent one. The subject refusals fell from 98 to 35 and changed class: names were the source's designators, and the 35 split into 14 genuinely unnamed, 7 aliases, 8 partial names, 6 whitespace artefacts of the text layer.

## 4. What the restriction bought and what it cost

Rows fell from 1,466 to 552 and the review from 1.21 million tokens to 498 thousand, with support unchanged in kind (542 of 552). Two questions went PARTIAL for one reason each, and it is the same reason: a required semantic sits on a source-asserted record that carries no subject. CQ-01's deployed-instrument count is an observation with no subject; CQ-04's extensional stress is a claim with no subject, as are the pore-pressure trigger and the gas-phase loss. Under run-09's binding those records were ENTITY rows and reachable; under v4.4 a source-asserted record reaches a question only through its subject, so they reach nothing. That is the honest outcome the journal asked for, priced: subject coverage is 49 per cent, and everything in the other 51 per cent is invisible to the questions.

The cure is coverage, not a return to the flood. Iteration 3's change (the check accepting an entity's tags and ignoring whitespace) targets the 21 refusals that were aliases, partial names and artefacts, and the sixteen subjects the producer dropped after the return were mostly of those classes. The residue after that is the class where the sentence names nothing, which the census should count and the review should read as the source's own limit.

## 5. Two defects in the review surface

The task's derivation-locality token was defined for relation rows (the relation's block among its endpoints' blocks) and applied to SUBJECT rows, where the subject entity is introduced once and referred to everywhere; 232 of 552 rows were tokened NON_LOCAL for that reason alone. For a SUBJECT row the subject check is the locality (the name occurs in the record's own formalizing statement), so the token adds nothing there. Less: drop the locality token for SUBJECT and ENTITY rows; keep it for RELATION rows.

Fault status fields (ACTIVE, INACTIVE) asserted flat where the reading hedges: the Fault type carries no modality, so the hedge has nowhere to go. This is the hedge-loss class again, in an enum instead of an interval; the value qualification solved it for numbers. A pack-level answer would be a modality on any source-asserted enum, which is decision 15's idea applied to categorical values.
