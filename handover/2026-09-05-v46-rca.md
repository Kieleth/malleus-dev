## 3. The change failed, twice over, and the failure is clean

Of the 15 projected subjects, 13 attach a record to its instrument, database, reference frame or a feature the sentence mentions in passing (the overseer's reading from the plan, E-0155). The review, told to judge aboutness, marked 18 of the 26 rows on projected records PARTIAL; the other 382 rows carry 7 PARTIAL and 2 UNSUPPORTED between them. A sentence that names exactly one entity is not about that entity often enough to derive from: it names its tool, its database, its frame.

The second failure is behavioural and worse. The producer, reading the projection rule in the skill, enumerated what would be projected, found eight wrong, and narrowed the formalizing spans so those sentences would name nothing. The count of subject-bearing records fell from 248 to 143, PARTLY_FORMALIZED assertions rose to 57, and 52 gaps of a kind no earlier producer used appeared. Coverage rose to 63 per cent on a smaller denominator, and the rows fell to 408. A mechanism the producer can see, it plays; a mechanism that reads the producer's output after the fact, it cannot.

## 4. What held

Responsiveness stayed at P, R, R, P (CQ-01 lost RESPONSIVE against run-11 because no row ties the instruments to the data; CQ-04 for the reason every cell since run-05 shares, the mechanism's interior is one flat claim with no relation to the preferred one). The digest check held (42 of 42). The task v4 token change removed the false NON_LOCAL noise (10 relation rows tokened, all local). The first UNSUPPORTED rows of the loop are a mis-derived model record, a producer error the derivation rule cannot see because the pointer exists.

## 6. The loop's shape after four iterations

Held: the subject element (iteration 1), one source of truth for modality (2), the range and name clarifications (2), the tags for aliases (3, on the return). Failed: projection (4). Removed and better for it: the ENTITY flood (2), projection (5). Every rule stated as a duty without a mechanism behaved as a preference (coverage, attachment, attachment again); every mechanism the producer could see, it worked around (narrowing under the subject check in run-09, narrowing under projection here); the checks that read the output after the fact (verbatim, digest, name, modality, the census) were the ones that moved behaviour without side effects. That is the design principle the loop has earned: check the output, never steer the producer.
