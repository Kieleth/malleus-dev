## Closed semantics

The instruction has exactly `opcode`, `values` and `refusal`. `values` resolves
one finite list of strings. Require strictly increasing lexicographic Unicode
code-point order, not locale or UTF-16 code-unit order. Equal strings refuse.
Empty and singleton lists pass this check only. Nonempty and nonblank field
requirements remain separate constraints of the selected record contract.

Validate the entire operand type before comparison. Malformed values refuse
using the declared refusal, without coercion. Do not sort, trim, normalize,
case-fold, transform or mutate anything. There is no result, state target,
comparator or callable. A future interpreter must implement this exact generic
operation or refuse the required instruction; no fallback to Assent handlers.
